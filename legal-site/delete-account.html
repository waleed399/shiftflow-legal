Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: May 22, 2026

This page explains how you can request the deletion of your Shift-Right account and the personal data associated with it. We provide two ways to request deletion: directly inside the app, or by email.

1. Delete Your Account In-App

Steps:
  1. Open the Shift-Right app on your device.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account and personal data will be removed within 30 days of the request.

2. Request Deletion by Email

If you can no longer access the app, you can request deletion by sending an email from the address associated with your Shift-Right account:

support@shiftright.app

Please use the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will verify your identity, process the deletion within 30 days, and confirm by email once it is complete.

3. What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the following:

  • Account profile (name, email, phone number, profile photo)
  • Authentication data (password hash, refresh tokens, device tokens)
  • Your shift assignments, availability, and time-off requests
  • Swap requests you have created
  • Push notification tokens registered to your account
  • In-app notifications addressed to you

4. What Data May Be Retained

For legal, accounting, and security reasons, we may retain a limited subset of data after account deletion:

  • Anonymized usage and crash logs (no personal identifiers) — retained per Sentry retention policy
  • Billing and payment records held by our payment processor (Lemon Squeezy) — retained up to 7 years to meet tax and accounting obligations
  • Webhook and audit logs that do not directly identify you — retained up to 90 days for security

5. Organization Data (For Managers)

If you are the manager of an organization, deleting your personal account does not automatically delete the organization, its workers, or its shift history. To delete an entire organization and all of its data, please email support@shiftright.app and identify yourself as the organization owner.

6. Reversibility

Account deletion is permanent. Once your data has been removed it cannot be restored. If you are unsure, consider signing out instead — you can sign back in at any time.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this process or need help with a deletion request, contact us at support@shiftright.app.
